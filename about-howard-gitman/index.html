A Life in Horology

About Howard Gitman

A time-honoured professional with more than forty years in antique watches, clocks, and related rarities.

Moving Time Around the World

Howard Gitman has spent more than forty years buying, selling, repairing, and appraising fine antique watches and clocks. Through Gitman Antique Watches & Rarities, he works with individual owners, families, collectors, and fellow members of the horological community.

His work extends beyond running timepieces. Howard is actively interested in unusual pocket and wrist watches, movements, watchmaker tools, clocks, enamels, music boxes, and related objects—including items that are incomplete or no longer working.

Knowledge Begins With Careful Attention

Every antique object arrives with its own combination of maker, materials, mechanics, condition, alterations, and history. Howard’s work begins by considering the particular item rather than treating every watch or clock as interchangeable.

Whether the goal is to understand a family piece, evaluate a collection, consider a sale, or discuss a repair, the first step is a direct conversation about what you have.
