Antique Pocket Watch Buyers & Appraisals

Gitman Antique Watches & Rarities is actively interested in unusual pocket watches, from individual heirlooms to broader collections.

An Experienced Look at Pocket Watches

Pocket watches bring together precision mechanics, decorative arts, metallurgy, and personal history. Howard Gitman has spent more than forty years buying, selling, repairing, and appraising antique watches and clocks.

A pocket watch does not need to be running or recently serviced to begin a conversation. Incomplete or non-working pieces may still be of interest, as may movements, cases, dials, chains, tools, and related material.

Understanding a Piece

Details That May Matter

Maker & Movement

Names, serial numbers, inscriptions, movement construction, and signatures can help identify a watch.

Case & Dial

Case marks, materials, decoration, dial condition, hands, and the relationship between components provide context.

History & Completeness

Boxes, papers, chains, repair notes, family history, and provenance may add useful information.

Before You Contact Us

Photographs That Help

01

Front & Back

Photograph the full watch, dial, hands, case, and exterior surfaces in even light.

02

Marks & Inscriptions

Include readable close-ups of visible names, numbers, hallmarks, engravings, and dedications.

03

Accessories

Show any chain, fob, box, papers, tools, spare parts, or accompanying material.

Do Not Force or Polish the Watch

If a cover will not open easily or the winding mechanism resists, leave it as it is. Aggressive cleaning or polishing can remove original surface information. Howard can advise what additional views or details may be useful after an initial conversation.
