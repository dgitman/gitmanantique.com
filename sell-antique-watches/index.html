Items We Are Actively Interested In

Gitman Antique Watches & Rarities is actively interested in buying unusual pocket and wrist watches, movements, watchmaker tools, clocks, enamels, and music boxes. A piece does not need to be running or in working order to be considered.

Condition is only one part of understanding an antique object. The maker, age, construction, rarity, provenance, and completeness may also be relevant when discussing a watch or collection.

Helpful Details Are Welcome, Not Required

If available, include photographs of the front, back, dial, movement, inscriptions, hallmarks, boxes, papers, and any history associated with the item. If you do not know the maker or age, simply share what you can see.

Do not attempt to force open a case, polish the metal, or make the item run before contacting Howard. Preserving an object as found can help retain information relevant to its evaluation.
